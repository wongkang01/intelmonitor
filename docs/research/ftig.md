# FTIG research: what a Fintech and Innovation Group profile would monitor

Researched 19 October 2026.
Sources are MAS pages, the MAS Financial Institutions Directory (FID), BIS and FSB pages, each opened during this research.
FID facts were read from the live directory on 19 October 2026 (it showed "Last Updated: 03 Oct 2026").
Anything marked **unverified** came only from search snippets or secondary reporting, or could not be confirmed on a primary page.

## Verdict

FTIG is a good next profile.
Its subject matter (stablecoins, crypto platforms, tokenisation, payments innovation, AI in finance) is news heavy, moves weekly, and is poorly covered by the current subject list.
One caveat shapes the profile: on the MAS organisation chart, the Payments department sits in the Policy, Payments and Financial Crime Group, not in FTIG ([MAS org structure](https://www.mas.gov.sg/who-we-are/organisation-structure)).
So Payment Services Act licensing and scam rules are mainly another group's job, while FTIG drives development work such as tokenisation, digital money, payments innovation and AI.
The profile below covers both, because FTIG staff follow the licensed firms that take part in their projects, and the news does not split along org lines.

## 1. What FTIG is

### Mandate and history

- MAS set up FTIG on 1 August 2015, "responsible for regulatory policies and development strategies to facilitate the use of technology and innovation" in the financial sector ([MAS 2015](https://www.mas.gov.sg/news/media-releases/2015/mas-sets-up-new-fintech-and-innovation-group)).
- At set-up it had three units: a Payments and Technology Solutions Office, a Technology Infrastructure Office (cloud, big data, distributed ledgers), and a Technology Innovation Lab ([MAS 2015](https://www.mas.gov.sg/news/media-releases/2015/mas-sets-up-new-fintech-and-innovation-group)).
- The current MAS organisation page lists FTIG under "Markets and Development" with no departments shown ([MAS org structure](https://www.mas.gov.sg/who-we-are/organisation-structure)).
- **Unverified:** the current internal units of FTIG. No page I opened names a "Fintech Infrastructure Office", a digital assets department or any other current FTIG sub-unit.
- MAS describes its fintech role as building "a Smart Financial Centre" across data, digital assets and AI ([MAS FinTech](https://www.mas.gov.sg/development/fintech)).

### Leadership roles

- FTIG is headed by the Chief FinTech Officer ([MAS 2024 GFTN release](https://www.mas.gov.sg/news/media-releases/2024/mas-announces-establishment-of-global-finance-and-technology-network)).
- A new Chief FinTech Officer took over on 1 February 2025, tasked with "payments innovation, asset tokenisation" and AI and quantum applications ([MAS 2024 GFTN release](https://www.mas.gov.sg/news/media-releases/2024/mas-announces-establishment-of-global-finance-and-technology-network)).
- The founding Chief FinTech Officer became Group CEO of the Global Finance and Technology Network (GFTN) and an Advisor to FTIG from the same date ([MAS 2024 GFTN release](https://www.mas.gov.sg/news/media-releases/2024/mas-announces-establishment-of-global-finance-and-technology-network)).
- GFTN replaces Elevandi and works with MAS on payments, tokenisation, AI and quantum, and on scaling the Singapore FinTech Festival ([MAS 2024 GFTN release](https://www.mas.gov.sg/news/media-releases/2024/mas-announces-establishment-of-global-finance-and-technology-network)).
- The Chief FinTech Officer was quoted in that role in the BLOOM launch on 16 October 2025 ([MAS BLOOM](https://www.mas.gov.sg/news/media-releases/2025/mas-launches-bloom-initiative-to-extend-settlement-capabilities)).
- MAS's Chief Technology Officer, who sits outside FTIG, fronted the quantum-safe sandbox report ([MAS QKD 2025](https://www.mas.gov.sg/news/media-releases/2025/mas-and-industry-partners-publish-technical-report-on-proof-of-concept-sandbox)).

### Priorities for 2025 to 2026

The MAS Managing Director's speeches at the Singapore FinTech Festival on 13 November 2025 and at INSEAD on 9 October 2026 set the agenda.

- **Digital assets:** move Project Guardian from pilots to commercial adoption, and orchestrate collective adoption such as tokenised repo ([MAS speech Oct 2026](https://www.mas.gov.sg/news/speeches/2026/building-trusted-foundations-for-digital-finance)).
- **Digital money:** settle tokenised MAS Bills with SGD wholesale CBDC, grow BLOOM, and legislate the stablecoin framework ([MAS speech Oct 2026](https://www.mas.gov.sg/news/speeches/2026/building-trusted-foundations-for-digital-finance)).
- **Digital infrastructure:** Global Layer One (GL1) standards and toolkits for a "network of networks" ([MAS speech Oct 2026](https://www.mas.gov.sg/news/speeches/2026/building-trusted-foundations-for-digital-finance)).
- **Agentic AI:** the SAFR (Safeguards for Agentic Finance at Runtime) white paper, version 1.1, and an open-source reference implementation ([MAS speech Oct 2026](https://www.mas.gov.sg/news/speeches/2026/building-trusted-foundations-for-digital-finance)).
- **AI adoption:** BuildFin.ai, PathFin.ai (over 100 participants), MindForge, and AI workforce upskilling ([MAS speech Nov 2025](https://www.mas.gov.sg/news/speeches/2025/creating-the-future-of-finance)).
- **Payments:** PayNow and SGQR usable in Thailand, Malaysia, Indonesia (QRIS) and India ([MAS speech Nov 2025](https://www.mas.gov.sg/news/speeches/2025/creating-the-future-of-finance)), and PayNow Generation 2 ([MAS PayNow Gen2](https://www.mas.gov.sg/news/media-releases/2026/mas-and-abs-chart-instant-payments-enhancements-with-paynow-generation-2-study)).
- **Quantum:** a new FI innovation centre for quantum-safe encryption ([MAS speech Nov 2025](https://www.mas.gov.sg/news/speeches/2025/creating-the-future-of-finance)).
- **Funding:** FSTI 4.0, S$220 million over three years, announced 31 August 2026 ([MAS FSTI 4.0](https://www.mas.gov.sg/news/media-releases/2026/mas-commits-s$220-million-to-support-next-phase-of-fintech-innovation)).

### Market infrastructure

- PayNow is an addressing scheme on FAST; SGQR has run since 2018; the PayNow and UPI link launched 21 February 2023 ([MAS e-Payments](https://www.mas.gov.sg/development/e-payments)).
- PayNow and SGQR work in Thailand, Malaysia, Indonesia (QRIS) and India; over 90% of Singapore businesses use PayNow or SGQR ([MAS speech Nov 2025](https://www.mas.gov.sg/news/speeches/2025/creating-the-future-of-finance)).
- PayNow, FAST, NETS, BCS, MEPS+ and GIRO are already subjects in `api/seed/subjects.yaml`.
- The SGD Testnet hosts wholesale CBDC for settling tokenised assets ([MAS speech Oct 2026](https://www.mas.gov.sg/news/speeches/2026/building-trusted-foundations-for-digital-finance)).
- Recognised market operators with a tokenisation or digital asset angle include ADDX, DBS Digital Exchange, DigiFT, SDAX, Chintai and Asia Digital Exchange ([FID RMO list](https://eservices.mas.gov.sg/fid/institution?category=Recognised%20Market%20Operator)).

## 6. Overlap and gaps against `api/seed/subjects.yaml`

### Already covered

- Entities: DBS, OCBC, UOB, MAS, HKMA, BNM, Bank of Thailand, OJK, SGX, HKEX, MEPS+, FAST, PayNow, GIRO, NETS, BCS, SWIFT, Visa, Mastercard.
- Topics: `cyber-threats` (includes deepfake fraud and post-quantum keywords), `tech-risk-regulation` (includes AI risk management), `tech-outages` (payment disruption), `banking-regulation` (AML/CFT).
- Groups: `payment-systems` already holds the domestic rails.

### Missing

- **Topics:** every FTIG topic in section 4 except quantum-safe. There is no topic for stablecoins, crypto, tokenisation, payments innovation or AI in finance.
- **Entities:** all stablecoin issuers, DPT providers, payment institutions, digital banks and standard setters in section 3. This confirms the earlier finding that OKX, Crypto.com (Foris), PayPal, StraitsX, Ant International and MetaComp are missing; Circle, Coinbase, Paxos, Grab, Stripe, Partior, GXS, MariBank, Trust Bank, Nexus Global Payments, BIS Innovation Hub and FSB are also missing.
- **Central banks:** RBI, Bank Indonesia and BSP (needed for Nexus); OJK is present but Bank Indonesia is not.
- **Entity kinds:** `EntityKind` in `api/intelmonitor/seed.py` has no kind for a payment institution, a crypto or digital asset firm, a stablecoin issuer or an international standard setter. `broker` appears in relationship endpoints but not as a subject kind. A profile needs at least `payment_institution`, `digital_asset_firm` and `standard_setter`, each with a label in `_KIND_LABEL` (`api/intelmonitor/pipeline/subjects.py`).
- **Endpoint kind for initiatives**, so participates_in edges to Guardian, BLOOM, GL1 and Orchid have a target.
- **Groups:** suggested `stablecoin-issuers`, `digital-asset-platforms`, `payment-institutions`, `digital-banks`, `standard-setters`.

# Monitor page

A monitor page is one topic's or entity's view as of the latest run.
The head shows the logo or icon, the name and one line: an entity's kind and its peers as links (`Bank · Peers: OCBC, UOB`, with `N more` when there are more than five), or a topic's scope; `link "Ask about <name>"` sits on the right.
Under the head, underlined tabs: Overview, Developments, Related (when something links to the subject) and Data (subjects with listed series).
The Overview holds the summary band full width (the week's brief, `region "This week"`, with the top 3 Key data figures, the next date and `Open Data` on its right), then the subject's top 5 developments this month and the Related card, each full width.
The Developments tab holds the full feed with its filters, under High, Medium and Low headings.
The Related tab (`/monitors/<id>/related`, components/monitor/Related.tsx) holds what reaches the subject through its links: `Could affect <name>` (banks always, others when something reaches them), the linked institutions for a country, sector, technology provider or payment system, and `Shared with` for a bank with peers.
It shows when the subject has impacts this week, linked institutions, or (a bank) peers; otherwise the tab is absent and its URL lands on the Overview.
Under 900px the band's figures go under the brief, side by side, and the Related card's list goes under its graph.
Groups have no page (decision S3): `/monitors/<group>` lists the members as links, and its `/data` URL lands there.
The Data tab is in [data.md](data.md); the Developments tab's feed is in [developments](developments.md); the full graph view (reachable by URL only) is in [graph.md](graph.md).

## Sub-features

- `head` `heading [level=1]` with the subject name; the line under it with peer links and `button "N more"`.
- `tabs` `navigation "Monitor views"` with `link "Overview"`, `link "Developments"`, `link "Related"` and `link "Data"`; the open tab has `aria-current="page"`.
- `brief` `region "This week"` (`section.ov-band`, components/monitor/overview/SummaryBand.tsx), its heading visually hidden: the direction (`Unchanged from last week` and the reason), then the state at 22px (one sentence on where the subject stands; on a quiet week it says there is nothing new to worry about and gives the standing position), then up to two concerns, each its bold label (`Property loans.`, absent on briefs written before labels) and a plain sentence (a risk or an opening and why it matters; an indirect one names its route, such as `through its Hong Kong loans`). No development links or titles, no counts line, no Related line. With no brief: `No summary for <name> this week.`
- `band-side` (`.ov-band-side`, the band's right column): the first 3 of the API's Key data pick (`key_data.flagged` then `key_data.rows`), each its name over the latest value and its change (`.kf[data-series-id]`), then `link "Open Data"` (only with figures), then `Next: <date>, <label>`, the soonest of the brief's and the next 90 days' dates, the label a link to its development with the development's title as its tooltip. The column is absent when there are neither figures nor dates, and the brief takes the band.
- Key data and Data tab changes are coloured by the series' bad direction: green when good, red when bad (a rise in an NPL or loan-to-deposit ratio is red).
- `related-card` `region "Related to <name>"` (components/monitor/RelatedGraph.tsx), under the top 5: only the linked nodes whose developments in the window reach the subject, as a graph around it (`data-testid=related-canvas` with `data-layout`), each with its count inside (`.rg-badge[data-node]`) and sized by it (5 developments at three times the radius of 1), red when one could hurt the subject and blue otherwise; `7 days`/`30 days`/`90 days` and the legend above. Beside the graph, `data-testid=related-list` holds each node's developments under its name (`.rg-group[data-node]`, a `region` named after the node), rows opening the development popup. A node click scrolls the list to its group and marks it (`data-selected`); hovering or focusing a group highlights its node. Empty window: `No news reached <name> through its links in the last <n> days.` `link "See all links"` to the Related tab, only when the subject has that tab (`mas`, `sgx` and other subjects with only inbound links show the card without it). Absent for a subject with no stored links (most topics).
- `top` `region "Top <name> developments"` for an entity, `region "Top developments"` for a topic: the first 5 rows of this month's feed, unconfirmed ones left out (see [developments](developments.md)), High and adverse for the subject first, then other High, Medium adverse, other Medium and the rest, newest first within each, each with its priority badge (no headings), then `link "See all <n> developments this month"` (or `See all developments` with 5 or fewer) to the Developments tab. Empty: `No developments this month.`, then `link "See earlier developments"` to the tab on All time (`?days=any`) when older ones exist, and no link when the subject has none; error: `These developments could not be loaded.` Rows open the development popup (`?development=<id>&scope=subject:<id>`).
- `developments-tab` `/monitors/<id>/developments`: `region "Developments about <name>"` with the full feed under the column header, the period and facets in the URL (`?days=7&priority=High`). A group's URL lands on its member list.
- `related-tab` `/monitors/<id>/related`: the sections below, top to bottom; no Overview panels. A group's URL lands on its member list; a subject with nothing related lands on its Overview.
- `could-affect` (Related tab) `region "Could affect <name>"`, meta `<n> this week` (the last 7 days of indirect impacts): headings by kind of link, `Exposure` (loans), `Ownership and structure` (owns, subsidiary of, shareholder of, operates in, member of), `Dependency` (suppliers and systems it takes part in), `Other links`; under each, the link in words (`Singapore loans, 44.4% of total`) with its evidence line, then up to 3 developments (`Could hurt`, `Could help` or `Mixed effect`, the title as a link, the full why, `News about <place>` on region-wide links, the date) and `button "Show N more"`. Empty, for banks only: `Nothing this week reaches <name> through its links.`
- `linked` (Related tab; countries, sectors, technology providers, payment systems) `region` titled `Institutions with loans or subsidiaries in <place>`, `Institutions lending to <sector>`, `Institutions that use <provider>` or `Institutions that take part in <system>`, meta `<n> institutions`, from `GET /subjects/<id>/linked`: one row per institution, its name (a link when tracked, else `No page yet`), each route in words with its evidence line, and `This week, through these links` with two developments and `button "Show N more"`. Region-wide figures follow under `Figures for a whole region that includes <place>`. 10 rows, then `button "Show N more"`. When five or more institutions share one single link from one source and have no news this week (FAST's 36, MEPS+'s 58), that link shows once after the other rows (`Each of these 58 takes part in MEPS+.`) over their names in columns. Then the evidence legend, with `Names without a link have no page yet.` when some have no page. Institutions are banks, insurers, brokers, asset managers and market infrastructure (exchanges, depositories, clearing houses), plus payment firms that take part in a payment system. The section shows only with at least one institution. Error: `These institutions could not be loaded.`
- `shared-with` (Related tab; banks with peers) `region "Shared with"`, `combobox "Peer"` (defaults to the first peer), meta `<n> links in common`, from `GET /shared`: a claim (`Both use AWS and Singtel. Both take part in FAST, ...`), column heads `<bank> · Both linked to · <peer>`, rungs grouped `Suppliers`, `Payment systems and markets`, `Exposures` (only where both disclose a share), `Other links`, each with the sources under it; 6 rungs then `button "Show N more"`. Empty: `<bank> and <peer> share no stored links.` Error: `These links could not be loaded.`
- `group` `/monitors/<group>`: `heading "<group>"`, `Open one of its members.`, one row link per member with its kind and a chevron. No tabs.
- `redirects` `/monitors/<id>/connections` on the Overview; `/monitors/<id>/trend` on Data; `/monitors/<group>/data` on the group's members.

## How to get to it (user POV)

- Sidebar `Monitors` trees (Topics, Groups, Entities): `link "<Subject>"` or `link "<Subject> <n> new"`. The Entities tree starts collapsed: click `button "Entities 43"` first.
- ⌘K search, type the name, Enter.
- Any subject link: a development row or the development popup, the head's peer links, a linked institution's name, a Shared with endpoint, a dashboard's watchlist row, the sidebar's Entities groups and Topics.
- The tabs in `navigation "Monitor views"`; the band's `Open Data`; the Overview's `See all ... developments`; the Related card's `See all links`.
- Direct URLs `/monitors/<id>`, `/monitors/<id>/developments`, `/monitors/<id>/related` and `/monitors/<id>/data`; hash links such as `/monitors/<id>/related#linked` or `#developments` scroll to that section once it renders.

## Driving it with verify

Preconditions:

- `pnpm -C web verify doctor` is ok.
- `S` below means `agent-browser --session $VERIFY_SESSION` (default `im-verify`).
- Harness targets: `verify open monitor <subject>`, `monitor-developments <subject> [query]` (the query sets the feed's period and facets), `related <subject>`, `data <subject>`, `graph <subject>`.
- Good subjects on 9 Oct: `dbs` (brief with two concerns, Key data, 27 impacts this week, Shared with OCBC and UOB), `uob` (33 impacts), `hong-kong` (no impacts, three linked institutions, UOB under the region heading), `microsoft-azure` (Could affect through `Operates in Singapore`, three institutions, no Data tab), `aws` (no Key data, six institutions, four without a page; the band's right column holds only `Next: 2 Nov, Applications close`), `fast` (36 institutions on one shared link), `meps-plus` (SGX with three routes, then 58 names on one shared link), `crowdstrike` (one institution, SGX through The Central Depository), `banking-regulation` (a topic: brief, one Key data figure, the top 5 full width, no Related card or tab), `singapore-banks` (a group).
- Opening a development marks it read in `development_triage`. Dump the table before a drive that opens one and delete the rows your visit added afterwards.

- **Open the page.** `pnpm -C web verify open monitor dbs`, `S snapshot -i`: `heading "DBS" [level=1]`, `link "OCBC"`, `link "UOB"`, `link "Overview"`, `link "Developments"`, `link "Related"`, `link "Data"`, `link "Ask about DBS"` (href `/ask?about=dbs`), `region "This week"`, `link "Open Data"`, `region "Top developments"`, `region "Related"`, and no Could affect or Shared with.
- **Brief.** `verify api /subjects/dbs/page` has `brief.state`, `brief.concerns[]` (`label`, `text` and the `development_ids` behind it, which the page does not show) and `upcoming`. The brief holds no development links: `agent-browser --session im-verify eval '(() => document.querySelectorAll(".ov-band-main a").length)()'` is 0. A brief stored before concerns (runs up to 8 Oct until they are rewritten) shows its old points' claims as concerns. Briefs written from the next run are built only from developments that pass the source gate, and mark single-outlet ones for the model to hedge (`reported by one outlet`).
- **Could affect.** Click the Related card's `link "See all links"`: `/monitors/dbs/related` opens with `aria-current="page"` on Related and `region "Could affect DBS"` first, under `Exposure` and `Ownership and structure`. Click a link group's `Show 5 more`; it goes and the rows grow. Click a title: the popup opens over the tab; Esc returns to `/monitors/dbs/related` at the same scroll.
- **Shared with.** On `/monitors/dbs/related`, `S select @<combobox "Peer"> UOB`: the claim becomes `Both use AWS and Singtel. Both take part in FAST, GIRO, MEPS+, Mastercard and 7 more.`; `verify api "/shared?a=dbs&b=uob"` has the same endpoints.
- **Linked institutions.** `verify open monitor hong-kong`, click the Related card's `link "See all links"`: `/monitors/hong-kong/related` shows `region "Institutions with loans or subsidiaries in Hong Kong"` alone: DBS and OCBC rows, then `Figures for a whole region that includes Hong Kong` and UOB. `Show 2 more` under DBS adds two developments. `link "DBS"` opens `/monitors/dbs`. `verify api /subjects/hong-kong/linked` returns the same three, DBS first.
- **Top developments.** Its rows are the first 5 of `verify api "/developments?subject_id=dbs&days=30&dismissed=false"`. Click `See all <n> developments this month`: `/monitors/dbs/developments` opens with `aria-current="page"` on Developments and `n` rows in the same order; `n` leaves out unconfirmed developments, and `Show unconfirmed` on the tab adds them.
- **Tabs.** Click `link "Related"`, `link "Developments"`, `link "Data"` in turn: each URL changes and `aria-current="page"` follows; `link "Overview"` returns, and Back steps through them.
- **Empty Related.** `/monitors/banking-regulation/related` lands on `/monitors/banking-regulation` with no Related tab; `/monitors/singapore-banks/related` shows the member list.
- **Filtered tab and group.** Open `verify open monitor-developments dbs "days=7&priority=High"`: `This week` is pressed, `Priority: High` shows and only the High heading remains. `/monitors/singapore-banks`, `/monitors/singapore-banks/developments` and `/monitors/singapore-banks/data` show the member list.
- **Error.** `/monitors/no-such-subject` shows `This page could not be loaded.` For the section errors, `S network route "**/linked*" --abort` (or `"**/shared*"`), reload, read the section, then `S network unroute`.
- **Proof.** `pnpm -C web verify shot <run>/<step>` per state, plus the `verify api` readback.

## Gotchas

- The page scrolls inside `main.main`, not the window: `S eval '(() => document.querySelector("main.main").scrollTo(0, 800))()'`, or scroll to a section: `document.getElementById("linked")`.
- `agent-browser snapshot` does not print `aria-current`. Read it with `S get attr @<ref> aria-current` or from the DOM.
- The app is light mode only; `agent-browser set media dark` changes nothing.
- The app shell keeps a 1024px minimum width; at 1024 the band's figures go under the brief, the top 5 and the Related card stack, and nothing scrolls sideways.
- Data tab internals are in data.md.

Simplified dashboard and shell

Three layouts for the fixed dashboard, then the New dashboard dialog. Click a dashboard in any sidebar to switch between Singapore banks and Technology and cyber risk.

Rows come from the 24 Sep update in the live store, with the last visit taken as 17 Sep and the feed set to the past month. Why lines are the brief's stored "why it matters" text or a stored indirect impact. Hatched lines mark rows with no stored why line yet.

Option A: feed with the watchlist beside it

The feed takes the wide column and reads top to bottom by priority. The watchlist and upcoming dates sit in a narrow column on the right, so the entity view is always in sight without pushing the feed down.

Dashboard

Option B: watchlist table on top, feed below

A compact table answers "anything new on my entities?" in one glance before the feed starts. The feed then runs nearly full width, with upcoming dates in a slim column.

Dashboard

Option C: one column, by entity and topic

No separate feed. Each entity, then each topic, is one row with its top development in full; opening it lists that subject's developments by priority. Reads like a briefing note, at the cost of a development about two banks appearing under both.

# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Supervisory and policy analysts at a financial regulator.
Each follows a few topics, sectors and institutions every week, on a desktop or laptop in an office, often with several other tools open.
Morning check: open a dashboard and know in under a minute what changed and what needs attention.
Check a development: read a short summary, open the sources, add a note.
Set up monitoring: describe what to watch and get something useful straight away.
Hand over: mark items reviewed and leave notes a colleague can pick up.

## Product Purpose

IntelMonitor pre-answers standing questions over a curated corpus and shows the answers, with every claim linked to its sources.
It replaces feed-reading with answers: a widget says "Regulatory: Stable to Watch", not 20 headlines.
Success is an analyst trusting the summaries enough to act on them, and clicking through to evidence when it matters.

## Brand Personality

Calm, exact, trustworthy.
Reads like a well-run internal tool an analyst learns once and stops noticing.
Color only means status. Neutral surfaces carry the rest.
Nothing decorative, nothing that moves on its own.

## Anti-references

- AlphaSense dashboards: dated, blank grid to start, feeds the analyst must read.
- SaaS dashboard templates: hero metrics, identical card grids, gradient accents, pill chrome.
- Consumer app softness: oversized corner radii, floating glassy panels, playful motion.
- Anything that looks generated: uniform padding everywhere, every element in its own rounded box, decorative icons.

## Design Principles

1. Answers, not feeds. Every surface leads with the state and what changed.
2. Changes first. Every page opens with what is new since the analyst last looked.
3. Evidence one tap away. No dead-end summaries.
4. Calm by default. Neutral surfaces; color is reserved for New, Watch, Needs attention and Stable.
5. Earned familiarity. Standard patterns, one component vocabulary, density where the data needs it.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Text contrast at least 4.5:1 (3:1 for text 24 px and up).
- Everything works by keyboard: visible focus ring, logical tab order, Esc closes sheets and drawers.
- Touch and pointer targets at least 44 px.
- Status is never color alone: badges always carry words.
- Reduced-motion settings turn all motion off.
- Desktop from 1280 px down to tablet width; phone is later.
